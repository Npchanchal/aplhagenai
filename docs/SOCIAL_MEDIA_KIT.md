## 1. Compliance guardrails (every platform, every post)

- Factual research tool — **not investment advice**, not SEBI RA content (counsel determination pending; see [customer/COMPLIANCE.md](customer/COMPLIANCE.md)).
- Never: Buy/Hold/Sell, price targets, "tips", "signals", "beat the market", returns vs Sensex/Nifty, stock-of-the-week.
- Never name competitors in public posts or bios.
- Coverage claims must match the labeled cohort (Sensex-depth hand-labeled; say so honestly).
- Company-specific posts: show the evidence trail (guidance quote → actual → source), no verdict language beyond met/missed/dropped/pending.
- Every bio carries "Not investment advice."
- Do not run Telegram/WhatsApp "stock groups" or finfluencer partnerships.

## 3. Launch posts (first week)

**LinkedIn / X, launch:**
> Management guidance is usually remembered anecdotally: "they always miss margins," "they deliver on volumes."
>
> CiteAlpha turns that into a record. The Guidance Credibility Index tracks quantified guidance against later actuals, and every point links to the dated statement and its NSE/BSE/IR source.
>
> Not sentiment. Not a rating on the stock. A delivery history you can cite in an IC memo.
>
> Sensex-depth hand-labeled coverage today; pilots open for Indian equity desks → citealpha.com/pilot
>
> Research tool, not investment advice.

**LinkedIn, definition post:**
> GCI ≠ sentiment.
> Sentiment asks: how did management sound?
> GCI asks: did management deliver what it quantified?
> Keep them on separate lines of the memo, so tone never overrides the guidance-vs-actuals table.

**LinkedIn, product-truth post:**
> Every company in CiteAlpha carries a data-quality badge: `hand_labeled` or `demo_structured`. We'd rather show you exactly what's verified than overstate coverage.

**YouTube, first video (2–3 min):** "What is a Guidance Credibility Index?" A screen recording: Tracker → company dossier → evidence trail → source document. Put the disclaimer card at the start and in the description.
