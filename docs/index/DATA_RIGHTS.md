# Data rights — public surfaces (W8.7)

**Decision (2026-09-29):** vendor prices (FMP and any EOD tape) are not shown on public or guest pages. GCI does not use prices as an input. Workbench may still fetch a tape behind `analytics_experimental` and an *Experimental — synthetic inputs* label until a commercial licence is in place.

**Link-out:** filings and transcripts stay on the issuer or exchange site. CiteAlpha stores a dated quote and a URL. We do not redistribute original PDFs on public pages.

Logged on Trust (`compliance.link_out_policy`, `prices_on_public: false`).
