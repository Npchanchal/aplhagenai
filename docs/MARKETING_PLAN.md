# CiteAlpha Marketing Plan (18 months)

**Product:** CiteAlpha Guidance Credibility Index (GCI)  
**Entity:** Ocotillo Innovation Private Limited  
**Beachhead:** Indian institutional equity research (buy-side / PMS / AIF / sell-side)  
**Constraint:** Factual research only — no Buy / Hold / Sell, no retail tip marketing.

---

## 1. Positioning

| Do say | Don’t say |
|---|---|
| Evidence-linked management guidance vs delivery | Stock tips / trade signals |
| Credit score for guidance accountability | “Beat the market” |
| India-first Sensex → Nifty depth | “Full Nifty 500 labeled today” unless true |
| Complements your market terminal | Replaces Bloomberg / AlphaSense |

**One-liner:** *A credit score for management guidance — with citations.*

**Category:** Guidance credibility / management delivery analytics (not sentiment).

---

## 2. ICP & messaging pillars

### Primary ICP (next 12 months)
1. **Buy-side / PMS / AIF** — IC memos, chronic miss screening  
2. **Sell-side associates** — auditable guidance-vs-actuals tables  
3. **Quant / platform** — PIT GCI API (smaller count, higher ACV)

### Message pillars
1. **Evidence** — every score point has a trail  
2. **India spine** — NSE/BSE + IR + concall reality  
3. **HITL** — Accept/Edit/Reject before GCI math  
4. **Compliance posture** — research tool, not advice  

---

## 3. Channel mix (priority order)

| Channel | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder-led outbound** | Pilots (15–20 desks) | Weekly target list |
| **SEO + blog** | Inbound education | 2 posts/month after initial 20 |
| **LinkedIn** | Category education + pilot CTAs | 3–4 posts/week |
| **Customer pack** | Sales enablement | Always current |
| **Webinars / desk demos** | Conversion | 1× / month once pilots exist |
| **Partnerships** | Broker research / IR tools / EM platforms | Opportunistic Year 1–2 |
| **Paid ads** | Optional LinkedIn ABM only | After messaging proven |

Paid consumer CAC is **out of scope**. Capital goes to labeling depth + enterprise sales, not retail ads.

## 4. SEO & content program

### Live now
- Technical SEO: `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, canonicals, per-route titles/OG, Organization/WebSite/BlogPosting JSON-LD  
- Content: **20** research blog posts at `/blog`  
- Public URLs indexed: landing, Tracker, Desk, Research, Package, Rankings, About, Help, Trust, Blog  

### Keyword themes (India + institutional)
- guidance credibility / management guidance vs actuals  
- earnings guidance tracker India  
- concall guidance extraction  
- point-in-time fundamental alt-data  
- SEBI-safe research tooling (educational, not advice)

### Cadence after launch
| Month | Content |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Amplify the 20 posts; 1 case-style workflow article from pilot feedback |
| 3–6 | Sector explainers (banks, IT, auto) using **public method**, not stock picks |
| 6–12 | API/PIT developer notes + Trust Center deep dives |

### Technical follow-ups (ops)
1. Submit `https://citealpha.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster  
2. Verify OG image renders in LinkedIn/Twitter inspectors  
3. Consider prerender or SSR for blog HTML if crawl depth becomes an issue (SPA limitation)  
4. Add `hreflang` only when localized blog copies exist — don’t fake them

## 5. Demand generation calendar (quarterly)

### Q1 — Credibility
- Ship Sensex-depth narrative + Trust Center  
- 15 outbound pilot conversations/week  
- Publish blog cluster; LinkedIn carousels from posts 1–5  

### Q2 — Habit
- Weekly desk workflow webinars (30 min)  
- Convert 30–40% of warm pilots to paid seats  
- First API evaluation with one EM/quant prospect  

### Q3 — Proof
- Anonymized “delivery history” research notes (no recommendations)  
- Expand coverage messaging only as labeling allows  
- Case studies (permissioned) on review-queue time saved  

### Q4 — Scale sales
- Formalize Desk vs Enterprise packages  
- Partner conversations (data platforms)  
- Japan/EM localization only if India ARR supports it

## 6. Outbound playbook (founder-led)

**Target list:** top PMS/AIF + sell-side India coverage desks + India-dedicated EM PMs.  

**Sequence (email + LinkedIn):**
1. Problem: guidance remembered anecdotally  
2. Proof: one anonymized evidence-trail screenshot  
3. Offer: time-boxed pilot org + API key  
4. Ask: coverage list — reply with honest labeled vs demo status  

**Assets:** [ONE_PAGER](customer/ONE_PAGER.md), [COMPLIANCE](customer/COMPLIANCE.md), [PRICING](customer/PRICING.md), Package page, Trust Center, Blog.

**CTA:** `sales@citealpha.com` · in-product Package · `/register` for self-serve pilots.

## 7. Social / LinkedIn content formulas

1. **Definition** — “GCI ≠ sentiment”  
2. **Workflow** — Monday scan / dossier / watchlist  
3. **Compliance** — research vs advice  
4. **Product truth** — hand-labeled vs demo badges  
5. **India angle** — why local IR formats matter

## 8. Metrics (leading → lagging)

| Funnel | KPI |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Organic sessions to `/`, `/blog`, branded search |
| Interest | Package page views, Trust Center views, demo requests |
| Pilot | Active pilot orgs, weekly Desk DAU |
| Revenue | Paid seats, API ACV, logo retention |
| Moat | Hand-labeled statement count, Accept/Reject volume |

North star near-term: **weekly habit inside pilot desks**, not vanity traffic.
