**Bundle:** CiteAlpha Score · **Legal:** Ocotillo Innovation Private Limited

## What it is

**Guidance Credibility Index (GCI)** — a 0–100 score of whether Indian listed-company management delivered on quantified guidance, plus **peer delivery benchmarks** (sector/index percentiles for met / miss / drop rates).

Every score links to period, metric, guided band, actual, label, and source.

## Who it’s for

| Buyer | Job |
|---|---|
| Buy-side / PMS / AIF | Screen chronic guidance misses; size conviction with a track record |
| Sell-side research | Cite auditable guidance-vs-actuals in notes |
| Sector analysts | Compare delivery rates vs peers — not stock picks |
