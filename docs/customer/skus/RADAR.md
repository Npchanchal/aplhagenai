# CiteAlpha Radar — One Pager

**Bundle:** CiteAlpha Radar · **Legal:** Ocotillo Innovation Private Limited

## What it is

**Guidance Change Radar** — a near-term feed when management **revises ranges, drops a metric, withdraws guidance, or posts a material miss** — with links into evidence. Complements Score (historical delivery) with **change awareness**.

Also covers QoQ **diff briefs** and **event calendar hooks** as the SKU deepens.

## Who it’s for

| Buyer | Job |
|---|---|
| Portfolio managers | Catch guidance shifts without rereading every transcript |
| Risk / IR-watch | Monitor withdrawals and restatement flags |
| Associates | Start the week from a change inbox, not a blank search |

## What’s included (P1)

- Unified radar feed (`GET /api/radar/feed`) composing alerts + audit flags
- Severity, period, metric, source URL when available
- Deep links to company dossier / citations

## Roadmap (P2+)
