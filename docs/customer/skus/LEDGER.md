# CiteAlpha Ledger — One Pager

**Bundle:** CiteAlpha Ledger · **Legal:** Ocotillo Innovation Private Limited

## What it is

**Promise Ledger / Accountability Dossier** — for each company: what was promised, by whom, when, status (open / met / exceeded / missed / dropped), and source links. Score is optional; many buyers want the **audit trail**, not a 0–100.

Extends to **IR Mirror** (corporate), **board briefs**, and **credit-adjacent** commitment watches.

## Who it’s for

| Buyer | Job |
|---|---|
| Compliance / research governance | Evidence pack for notes and IC |
| Credit analysts | Track stated floors / capex / margin commitments vs delivery |
| IR / CFO (IR Mirror) | See how an accountability ledger would read externally |
| Independent directors | Quarterly open / missed / dropped pack |
